# Interview Rounds

Use these rounds in order. Do not collapse them into one long message unless the user explicitly
asks for that style.

## Round 1: Problem and Scope

- What problem are we solving
- Who experiences the problem
- What is inside the system boundary
- What is explicitly outside the first release

## Round 2: Outcomes and Constraints

- What business outcomes matter most
- What would success look like
- What constraints matter for timing, compliance, integrations, or operations

## Round 3: Actors and Use Cases

- Which humans interact with the system
- Which external systems interact with it
- What are the main user goals
- What is the normal flow for each goal
- What failure or exception paths matter

## Round 4: Requirements

- What must the system do
- What quality attributes matter
- What must be secure, fast, auditable, or easy to change

## Round 5: UCP Readiness

- Are actor complexities clear
- Are use-case complexities clear
- Are the major technical factors understood
- Are the major environmental factors understood

If the answer to any of these is no, stop and surface the missing points explicitly.
